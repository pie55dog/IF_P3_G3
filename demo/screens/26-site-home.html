Anand Family Farm offers local food and farm boxes for neighbors who want seasonal produce and a simple weekly pickup or delivery option. Our weekly harvest box is designed for regular customers who enjoy fresh food from a nearby farm.

Anand Family Farm

At Anand Family Farm, we grow and prepare weekly harvest boxes for local families and regulars who already know our farm. You can pick up during set weekly windows or choose local delivery in the neighborhoods we serve. Subscriptions repeat every week, and customers can pause a box when they need a break. If something is out of season, we can offer a waitlist or a substitute box when available.

Subscription pauses

Customers can pause a weekly box anytime from their account. If they go out of town, they are not charged for the paused week.

Billing and failed payments

Weekly charges are taken before each box. If a payment fails, we retry automatically and notify the customer.

Pickup and missed pickup windows

Pickup windows are set each week. If a pickup is missed, the box may be donated or discarded after a short cutoff.

Local delivery area

For local delivery, customers enter their address so we can check whether it is in our service area.

Changes, cancellations, and schedule updates

Cancellations require two weeks' notice. Plan changes start with the next cycle. On holidays or farm days off, customers may choose to skip or receive a substitute box.

Refunds and quality concerns

If an item arrives damaged or spoiled, customers should send a photo first. Refunds or store credit may be issued at the owner’s choice.

Accounts, emails, and reviews

An account is optional for buying. Customers receive order confirmation emails. Reviews and photos may be approved before they appear.

Tax and payment details

Sales tax handling needs owner confirmation. Payment is currently noted as card reader at a market or stall, but the checkout setup and payment flow need owner confirmation before going live.
